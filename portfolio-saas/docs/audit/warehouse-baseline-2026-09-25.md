# Portfolio and research warehouse baseline — 2026-09-25

This is an evidence ledger for the running VPS at `45.139.10.12` and the implementation branch `codex/trustworthy-research-foundation`. It is a baseline, not a claim that the financial warehouse is already suitable for investment decisions. The complete public-table column inventory is [production-schema-2026-09-25.csv](production-schema-2026-09-25.csv). That inventory contains names and types, no account records or secrets.

## Product boundary

Preserve the Activity ledger and the Portfolio, Net Worth, Allocation, Holdings, and Performance views. The current Markets, Guidance, risk, optimization, and operations surfaces are candidates for consolidation or removal after their data dependencies and users are checked. A successful rebuild means a user can ask an ad hoc financial question, inspect the exact source and calculation behind every reported number, and see a clear refusal when the warehouse cannot support the answer. Personal portfolio state and the shared research warehouse remain separate.

The attached `financial_agent_warehouse_plan.md` is a proposal to test, not a schema specification to implement literally. In particular, a single USD rate applied to every statement line would mix stock and flow measures. IAS 21 distinguishes transaction-date rates for income and expenses from closing rates for balance-sheet items; a period average is only a practical approximation when rates do not fluctuate significantly. An economic free-market-USD comparison also needs its own explicit convention and may differ from an accounting presentation. Store the original Rial fact, rate series, source, and conversion method before publishing a USD-derived number. [IFRS IAS 21](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/pdf-standards/english/2024/issued/part-a/ias-21-the-effects-of-changes-in-foreign-exchange-rates.pdf?bypass=on)

## Recovery state

The 2026-09-25 encrypted database dump (2,807,377,392 bytes) is on the Mac and the external ADATA drive. SHA-256 matched the VPS copy; a full decrypt streamed successfully through `pg_restore --file=/dev/null`. The passphrase is stored separately on the Mac with mode 0600. This proves archive readability, not that PostgreSQL and TimescaleDB can restore and serve the application. A full isolated restore remains an explicit release gate. Codal object copying to the external drive is in progress and needs a verified completion manifest. The VPS has limited free space, so do not run an isolated restore there or claim off-host recovery complete prematurely.

## Target architecture and sequence

1. **Recovery and operating safety.** Finish and verify Codal object copy; complete an isolated database restore; automate off-host backup verification; separate a persistent broker from the evictable cache. Keep production on its current version until these checks pass.
2. **Trustworthy source layer.** Preserve immutable artifacts and parser runs. Certify monthly sales first, then income statements and balance sheets template by template. Model reporting periods, standalone/consolidated scope, corrections, units, source cells, and abstentions. Never equate a parsed number with a certified fact.
3. **Comparable market layer.** Resolve symbol identity and industry membership over time, corporate actions, adjusted prices, cash USD versus USDT, and daily FX coverage. Treat flow translation, balance-sheet translation, and investment returns as different operations. Publish a derived metric only with formula/version and source rows.
4. **Research workspace.** Deterministic tools retrieve and calculate; the model plans and explains only within the returned evidence. Unsupported questions yield missing-data explanations. One research run has an in-app, user-selected USD spend ceiling and a separate server-side daily ceiling. The GapGPT key, model, URL, and token-price settings are projected from the existing News project on the VPS; no key reaches the browser or repository. Reserve a conservative maximum before each provider call, record actual usage even if the answer is rejected, and show cost and evidence beside the answer.
5. **Product simplification.** Keep the protected portfolio views and Activity. Make company exploration and research the main shared-data path. Prune or merge other routes only after verifying that no distinct trusted workflow remains. Validate desktop/mobile use and run paired source-to-answer examples before release.

Release examples: (a) a monthly-sales answer links to the exact Codal cell and survives a corrected filing; (b) a five-year USD-growth query reports its FX convention and excludes missing dates; (c) a net-profit ranking refuses to run until statement line items and periods are certified; (d) portfolio performance still reproduces ledger cash flows, rights issues, and reversals; (e) a restored database plus Codal object sample can reproduce a displayed answer. No AI call is needed to fetch or calculate these facts.
